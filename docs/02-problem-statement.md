# StockFlow — Problem Statement

## 1. Background

Small and medium-sized businesses often rely on manual or disconnected methods to manage their inventory and daily business operations.

Common methods include notebooks, spreadsheets, calculators, and messaging platforms. While these approaches may be manageable for very small businesses, they can become difficult to maintain as the business grows and the volume of products and transactions increases.

As businesses expand, having accurate and accessible information about stock, sales, purchases, and suppliers becomes increasingly important for effective day-to-day operations.

## 2. Problem

Many small and medium-sized businesses lack a centralized system for managing their inventory and business transactions.

Using manual or disconnected systems can make it difficult to maintain accurate records, monitor stock levels, track sales and purchases, and manage supplier information effectively.

As a result, businesses may experience:

- Inaccurate or outdated stock records.
- Difficulty identifying low-stock products.
- Errors when recording sales and purchases.
- Limited visibility into business performance.
- Difficulty maintaining consistent supplier records.
- Time-consuming manual calculations and reporting.
- Limited control over who can access or modify business information.

## 3. Impact of the Problem

Inefficient inventory and business management can affect the day-to-day operations of a business.

Poor visibility into stock levels may lead to stock shortages, excess inventory, or difficulties planning purchases. Inaccurate sales and purchase records can also make it harder for business owners and managers to understand business performance.

When information is stored across multiple disconnected systems, retrieving and maintaining accurate records can become time-consuming and increase the likelihood of errors.

## 4. Proposed Solution

StockFlow will provide a centralized web-based platform for managing inventory and core business operations.

The system will allow authorized users to manage products, categories, suppliers, stock, sales, and purchases from a single platform.

It will also provide low-stock alerts, business reports, and role-based access control to help businesses maintain accurate records and ensure that users only access functionality appropriate to their responsibilities.

## 5. Expected Benefits

The implementation of StockFlow is expected to provide the following benefits:

- Improved accuracy of inventory records.
- Better visibility into current stock levels.
- Easier management of products, categories, and suppliers.
- More efficient recording of sales and purchases.
- Faster identification of products requiring restocking.
- Improved access to business performance information.
- Better control over user access and responsibilities.
- Reduced reliance on manual and disconnected record-keeping methods.

## 6. Project Outcome

StockFlow aims to provide small and medium-sized businesses with a reliable and centralized platform for managing inventory and essential business operations.

The completed system should enable businesses to maintain organized records, monitor stock movement, manage transactions, and access relevant business information through a single application.

The project will also demonstrate the practical application of full-stack software engineering principles, including backend development, frontend development, database design, REST API development, authentication, authorization, testing, and deployment.
