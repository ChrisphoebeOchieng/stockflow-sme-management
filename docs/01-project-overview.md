# StockFlow — SME Inventory & Business Management Platform

## 1. Project Overview

StockFlow is a web-based inventory and business management platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses.

The platform will provide businesses with a centralized system for managing products, inventory, sales, purchases, suppliers, and business reports.

The goal of StockFlow is to replace fragmented methods of managing business operations, such as notebooks, spreadsheets, and informal messaging platforms, with a structured digital solution.

## 2. Project Purpose

The purpose of StockFlow is to provide small and medium-sized businesses with an accessible digital platform for managing their day-to-day inventory and business operations.

The system will help businesses maintain accurate stock records, monitor sales and purchases, manage suppliers and products, and access useful business information through reports and dashboards.

## 3. Target Users

StockFlow is intended for small and medium-sized businesses that need a centralized system for managing inventory and daily business operations.

The platform may be used by businesses such as:

- Retail shops
- Boutiques
- Beauty and cosmetics businesses
- Electronics shops
- Hardware stores
- Small wholesalers
- Other inventory-based businesses

## 4. Core Features

StockFlow will provide the following core features:

- Product management
- Category management
- Supplier management
- Inventory tracking
- Sales management
- Purchase management
- Low-stock alerts
- Business reports
- User management
- Role-based access control

## 6. Project Goals

The primary goals of StockFlow are to:

- Centralize inventory and business information.
- Improve the accuracy of stock records.
- Simplify the management of sales and purchases.
- Provide clear visibility into stock levels.
- Help businesses identify products that require restocking.
- Provide useful business reports for decision-making.
- Implement secure role-based access control.
- Demonstrate professional full-stack software development practices.

## 7. Success Criteria

StockFlow will be considered successful when an authorized business user can:

- Create and manage products.
- Organize products into categories.
- Manage supplier information.
- Track inventory levels and stock movements.
- Record sales and purchases.
- Receive notifications for low-stock products.
- View relevant business reports.
- Access features according to their assigned role.
